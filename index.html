Privacy Policy — MatchUp Pro

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Data we collect: NONE

MatchUp Pro is a 100% offline app. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data, telemetry, usage statistics, crash reports or device identifiers.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves locally on your phone:

  • Your teams (Pokémon, moves, items, natures)
  • Your saved opponents
  • Your settings (language, tutorial)

This data never leaves your device. It is stored via AsyncStorage and accessible only to you. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

3. Internet connection

The app does not require internet access and does not communicate with any external server. It works fully offline.

4. Third-party services

No tracking, analytics, advertising or third-party services are integrated. No Google Analytics, Firebase, AdMob, Crashlytics or similar.

MatchUp Pro is completely free: there are no in-app purchases or payments of any kind.

5. "Send Feedback" feature

Tapping "Send Feedback" in Settings opens your default email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) with a pre-filled message. The email is sent through YOUR provider — we only receive it after you press Send. No automatic transmission.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect any data, neither from minors nor from adults.

7. Changes to this policy

Future app versions may update this policy. Substantial changes will be communicated in the Play Store release notes.
